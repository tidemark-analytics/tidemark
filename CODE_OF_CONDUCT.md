# Code of conduct

Be kind and assume good faith. We want everyone to be able to take part regardless of experience, background or identity.

- Be respectful; disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Don't publish other people's private information.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may ban people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or the email on a maintainer's profile.

This is a short adaptation of the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
